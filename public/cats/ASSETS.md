# Orange and Tabby cat artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original outputs are retained in the local Codex generated_images directory. App assets live here, with derived favicon, Apple icon, and Open Graph image in app/.

## Cover prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide website cover for a playful personal notes app. Two original mischievous cartoon cats: one round bright orange cat and one gray striped tabby, smug half-closed eyes, cheeky smiles, playfully sprawled beside a little notebook and pencil. Warm cream background, bold dark chocolate outlines, flat orange, butter yellow and gray colors, polished charming sticker illustration. Wide landscape 3:2 composition, cats grouped in center with generous cream space around edges, no text, no letters, no watermark. Friendly comic style, not photorealistic.

## Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Square app icon, two original cheeky cartoon cat faces snuggled together, an orange cat on left and a gray striped tabby on right, half-closed mischievous eyes, confident little smiles, thick dark chocolate outlines, clean flat sticker style, warm butter-yellow solid background. Close-up faces fill central 75% of square; wide safe margins for rounded app icon crop; bold simple recognizable at tiny sizes. No text, no lettering, no watermark. Match warm friendly orange and gray cat notebook brand.

Rebuild resized assets with `node scripts/build-cat-icons.mjs` from the project root. The favicon replaces the original starter favicon; its previous version remains in Git history.
